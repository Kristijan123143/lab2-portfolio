About me

Hi, my name is Kristijan Duspara and I'm a master student at Örebro University studying information security. I'm originally from Stockholm, but I moved to Örebro to study.

One childhood memory that has stayed with me is using a computer at home and being curious about how websites and games worked. I did not understand the code behind them at the time, but I liked the feeling of exploring something interactive and creative.

My interest in web development grew during this course, when we started working with HTML and CSS. Seeing how structure and styling can turn simple text into a real website made me want to improve my own web development skills.

My interests include working out, playing football with friends, and spending time with family and friends. At the end of this course, I hope to have a better understanding of web development and be able to create more complex websites.
